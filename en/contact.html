FAQ

Do you deliver?

Yes. Order by phone at +998 90 916 77 74 or +998 97 766 99 88, or via the Telegram bot @Olimpolvon_bot.

What time do you open?

Open daily from 06:00 to 23:00. Mornings are for soups and national dishes.

Do I need to book?

For groups and weekends, book ahead using the form on the site or by phone.

How can I pay?

Cash, card, bank transfer or instalments.

Can I buy meat to take home?

Yes, the restaurant has its own meat shop: horse-meat kazy and roulades, beef and lamb roulades, smoked chicken.

Can we hold a celebration?

Yes — call us or leave a request marked “celebration” and the manager will suggest a menu and sharing portions.
