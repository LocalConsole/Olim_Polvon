A family restaurant of national cuisine

Olim Polvon Milliy Taomlari is where families come for norin, qovurma lagman and kebabs. Uzbek, Uyghur and European dishes on one menu.

Cuisine

The heart of the menu is what Tashkent goes to a milliy taomlar place for: norin, shilpildoq, halim, jarkob, ilik shorva and nokhat shorak.

The Uyghur side is all about lagman. The noodles are pulled by hand, and guests call qovurma lagman the restaurant's signature dish.

Kebabs are grilled over charcoal from halal meat: takhta, napoleon, Caucasian, Gijduvan minced, chicken and fish.

Meat shop

The restaurant runs a meat shop: horse-meat kazy and roulades, beef and lamb roulades, smoked chicken.
